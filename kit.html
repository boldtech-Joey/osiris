A small shop of things worth buying once. We stock a few makers we'd stand behind at the bench, in the sizes and colours we'd actually wear, and nothing else.

Waxed cotton jacket

The Bedale and Beaufort are the reason the bench exists. Wax cotton wears out slowly and forgivingly, and every part of one can be replaced or renewed.

Filson

Tin cloth cruiser

Oil-finish tin cloth is the American cousin of waxed cotton: heavier, stiffer, and it takes decades to give up. The Mackinaw wool is the same idea in a different material.

Stone Island

Garment-dyed shell

The odd one out here. Not built for the bench, but built well, and the fabric research is real. It earns its place on how long the good pieces actually last, not on repairability.
